Compare fever and scifact in terms of domain, claim construction, evidence unit. Can their reported scores be compared directly?

现有参考答案

FEVER constructs claims by mutating Wikipedia sentences and requires evidence sentences for supported or refuted claims. SciFact uses expert written scientific claims derived from citation sentences and labels individual claim and abstract pairs with rationale sentences. Their original scores concern different corpora, tasks, and evaluation settings, so they are not a controlled comparison of system quality.
